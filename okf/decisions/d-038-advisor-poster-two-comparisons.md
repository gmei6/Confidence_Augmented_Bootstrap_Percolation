# D-038 — Poster with two controlled comparisons; publication route resolved

**Decision (advisor-directed, meeting 2026-07-22):** The next deliverable is a poster built
on two controlled comparisons at matched n and average degree, kept separate: (1) degree
heterogeneity — Erdős–Rényi vs configuration model; (2) geometry — configuration model vs
GIRG with matched degree distribution (plus ER vs GIRG at matched mean degree). ER is the
baseline, justified via Janson. Optional: hard RGG dimension sweep. The poster must include
an ISyE-style recommendation/intervention component. Publication route: push to the frontier
(theoretical or empirical) after ~6 months; empirical leg via a SNAP dataset applied to the
existing framework.

**Rationale:** Advisor judged existing material sufficient for a poster; the two-comparison
design isolates the project's two causal claims (heterogeneity, geometry) cleanly. Resolves
the publication-route open question (§9 logistics) and implicitly greenlights GIRG runner
integration ("the most general model").

**Affects:** §9 (publication-route question closed), §10 (new task queue), roadmap
direction; supersedes nothing — extends D-028's advisor-track direction. Session: S-060.
