## 1. State of the art in one page (what is proven vs. what is open)

**Proven (Tier 1, D-025 — exact at finite $n$).** For any fixed $m$-tuple $S$ of non-seed nodes, the activation times $(Y_i')_{i\in S}$ are *exactly* conditionally independent given the leave-$S$-out fear-field trajectory $\mathbf g^{(-S)}$, with closed-form conditional survival factors (invariants I1–I3 of §5.1 in the reformulation doc). The fear field is a latent common factor: fix it, and all inter-node dependence vanishes.

**Open (Tier 2 — the Asymptotic Decoupling Conjecture, as sharpened by D-025).** Unconditional independence needs two more ingredients (reformulation doc, "Gap to Unconditional Independence"):
1. **Fear-field concentration** — the random functional $\mathbb E_f[h(t,\mathbf g^{(-S)},f)]$ concentrates around its mean as $n\to\infty$;
2. **Leave-out field equivalence** — $\mathbf g^{(-S)}$ for different small $S$ (and the full field $\mathbf g$) converge to one deterministic limit.

**Empirical status (S-029).** The excess variance ratio $R(\mu{>}0)-R(\mu{=}0)$ at round $t{=}3$ does **not** clearly vanish over $n\in\{1000,\dots,8000\}$ ($\mu{=}0.3$: $0.497\to0.413$; $\mu{=}0.5$: $0.750\to0.796$); distant-pair covariances are consistent with zero but equally underpowered. The walkthrough's own verdict: the design cannot distinguish slow decay from persistence.

**The precise gap, stated so it cannot be conflated** (task DoD item): D-025's conditional independence is a statement about what happens *once the field is fixed* — it is true at every $n$ and involves no limit. The conjecture is a statement about *the field itself*: that the random trajectory $(g_k)$ has vanishing fluctuations around a deterministic limit. The first is a factorisation of a conditional law; the second is a concentration-of-measure claim about the conditioning object. Conditional independence given a **random** common factor does not imply approximate unconditional independence unless that factor concentrates — that is the entire remaining content of the conjecture, and nothing in D-025 touches it.

## 2. The direct route: a variance-propagation sketch, taken as far as it goes

**Setup.** Work in the FIFO generational formulation. Let $\mathcal F_{k-1}$ = the sigma-algebra of the first $k-1$ generations. Two exact structural facts do real work:
(a) *Conditionally on $\mathcal F_{k-1}$, generation $k$'s size is a sum of independent Bernoullis* — solvency indicators use disjoint private edge collections $\{I_i(s)\}$, fear indicators use private uniforms $U_{i,k}$ against the $\mathcal F_{k-1}$-measurable field $g_k$. Hence the *within-generation* noise is binomial: $\mathrm{Var}(a_k\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}) \le \mathbb E[a_k\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}]$.
(b) The law of total variance then gives the propagation recursion
$$\mathrm{Var}(a_k) \;=\; \underbrace{\mathbb E\big[\mathrm{Var}(a_k\mid\mathcal F_{k-1})\big]}_{\le\, \mathbb E[a_k]\ \text{(Poisson-scale)}} \;+\; \underbrace{\mathrm{Var}\big(\mathbb E[a_k\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}]\big)}_{\text{fluctuations inherited from the past}}.$$

**Linearised inheritance.** Writing $m_k := \mathbb E[a_k \mid \mathcal F_{k-1}]$ as a smooth function of $(T_{k-1}, a_{k-1})$ and expanding around the mean-field trajectory, fluctuations obey (to first order) an AR-type recursion
$$\delta a_k \;\approx\; \lambda^{\mathrm{solv}}_k\,\delta T_{k-1} \;+\; \mu\,\delta a_{k-1} \;+\; \xi_k,\qquad \xi_k = \text{the binomial noise of (a)},$$
where $\lambda^{\mathrm{solv}}_k$ is the solvency map's local gain and the $\mu\,\delta a_{k-1}$ term is fear's contribution — the field passes last generation's fluctuation to every solvent node with total gain $\sum_i f_i/n \approx \mu$. Two conclusions drop out:

- **Fear adds no new divergence mechanism; it multiplies the baseline.** The fear gain is $\mu<1$, a strict contraction on its own (this is the subcritical-amplifier fact, §3.3, in fluctuation form). Summing the geometric propagation, fear inflates trajectory variance by a bounded factor of order $1/(1-\mu)^2$ relative to $\mu=0$. The *excess* relative variance therefore scales like $\big[(1-\mu)^{-2}-1\big]\cdot v^{(0)}_k$, where $v^{(0)}_k$ is the $\mu{=}0$ baseline relative variance.
- **Provable-looking regime.** If the *total* local gain stays uniformly below 1 — i.e. the seed is strictly subcritical, $a \le (1-\varepsilon)a_c(\mu)$, so the combined map's derivative is $\le 1-\delta(\varepsilon)$ along the whole (finite) trajectory — the recursion contracts, and $\mathrm{Var}(a_k)/\mathbb E[a_k]^2 = O(1/\mathbb E[a_k])$ by induction, with $\mathbb E[a_k] \asymp a_c \asymp n^{(r\alpha-1)/(r-1)} \to\infty$ in the Janson regime ($n^{0.4}$ at $r{=}2,\alpha{=}0.7$). Chebyshev then yields fear-field concentration *in the strictly subcritical phase, for any fixed number of generations*. The rigorous ingredients this needs are exactly the Tier-1 lemmas D-009 already sanctions (fear-only subcriticality; the $1/(1-\mu)$ bounded-amplification stochastic-dominance bound in `q1_rigor_assessment.md` §3), plus routine bookkeeping of the linearisation error — call this **Lemma C1**. Assessment: a real but *bounded* proof effort (days, not weeks), suitable for an appendix.

**The genuine obstruction (why this does not prove the conjecture where it matters).** At the critical seed — the regime the sharp-threshold program actually cares about — the combined map is *tangent to the identity by definition* of $a_c$: the local gain passes through $1$, fluctuation propagation is neutral, and the Poisson-scale noise injected each generation accumulates instead of contracting. The number of near-tangent "bottleneck" generations diverges at criticality (saddle-node slowing-down), so the accumulated relative variance does **not** vanish along this route. This is not a technique gap one more estimate fixes; it is the fluctuation-side face of critical slowing down, the same mathematical spot where Janson et al. need their heaviest martingale machinery even *without* a global coupling.

**And a defect in the conjecture's current wording that the obstruction exposes (new observation).** At $a = a_c(1+o(1))$ the outcome is *bimodal* — $P(\text{systemic})$ is bounded away from $0$ and $1$ (this is the project's own verified bimodality, Task B/§8). Conditional on the phase, the trajectories $(a_k)$ follow two macroscopically different courses. Therefore **the field cannot concentrate around a single deterministic trajectory at the critical seed — the conjecture as literally stated in §5 of the reformulation doc ("the sequence $(a_k)$ concentrates around its deterministic mean-field trajectory") is *false at criticality*, cheaply and rigorously** (a concentrated $(a_k)$ would force a degenerate systemic-event indicator, contradicting bimodality). It was always implicitly meant phase-conditionally; the fix is to say so:

> **Restated conjecture (ADC′).** For every $\varepsilon>0$: (i) for seeds $a \le (1-\varepsilon)a_c(\mu)$, the fear-field trajectory $(g_k)_{k\le K}$ concentrates around the (subcritical) deterministic mean-field trajectory as $n\to\infty$; (ii) for seeds in the critical window, the same concentration holds *conditionally on the non-percolation event* (and, dually, around the supercritical trajectory conditionally on percolation); (iii) the leave-$S$-out fields $\mathbf g^{(-S)}$, $|S|=O(1)$, concentrate around the same limits.
> Part (i) is Lemma-C1-provable per the sketch above. Parts (ii)–(iii) remain genuinely open.

**On (iii), and the withdrawn-approach warning (task watch-out).** The tempting route — bound the pathwise discrepancy between the full and leave-$S$-out cascades — is *exactly the discrepancy-bound argument that was found false and withdrawn pre-commit* (D-025 log: "unbounded cascading effects near the saddle-node tangency"). It is not re-derived here. The salvageable version again lives only in the strictly subcritical phase: there, a discrepancy (one extra/missing activation) spawns a subcritical descendant process, and the Tier-1 domination bound caps its total progeny at $O(1/(1-\mu))$ in expectation, giving $\|\mathbf g - \mathbf g^{(-S)}\|_\infty = O(|S|/n)$ *in that phase only* — i.e. (iii) merges into (i) away from criticality and inherits the identical obstruction at it.

## 3. The scope-out route: what the write-up honestly says if ADC′ stays open

The Tiered Stance (D-009) already places the combined-model sharp threshold at Tier 2 (conjecture + mean-field analysis), so leaving ADC′ open is *consistent with the project's declared rigor contract* — nothing previously promised is walked back. The honest write-up package is:

1. **Tier 1 results, stated as theorems:** the exact conditional factorisation (D-025) — genuinely novel and self-contained; fear-only subcriticality; bounded amplification; plus Lemma C1 if §2's sketch is executed (even C1 stated *as a proposition with proof sketch* materially upgrades the story: "decoupling is proven off-criticality; at criticality it is exactly as hard as the classical sharp-threshold problem").
2. **ADC′ stated as the open conjecture,** with the bimodality argument given as the *reason* for the conditional form — this preempts the sharpest referee attack (`q1_adversary_review.md` Scenario C: mixed rigor invites "why not both?"), because the paper itself exhibits the counterexample to the naive form and explains precisely what remains.
3. **S-029 reported as an underpowered-but-consistent finding:** "the excess variance ratio is consistent with the slow $O(1/a_c(n)) = O(n^{-0.4})$ decay predicted by the §2 propagation picture — a factor $\approx2.3$ over the tested $8\times$ range of $n$, within the observed CIs — but the design cannot exclude a persistent component; resolving this needs $n\gtrsim10^5$." Note the propagation sketch *makes the empirical prediction quantitative*, which S-029's write-up lacked: the excess should fall by $\approx(n_2/n_1)^{0.4}$, testable.
4. **The pending empirical companion:** queue Task E (fear-trajectory concentration; currently blocked on the sandbox's missing Python stack — see `docs/queue/reports/task_e_BLOCKED.md`) measures $\mathrm{Var}(g_k)/\mathbb E[g_k]^2$ vs $n$ directly and is the right instrument for ADC′(i); its resume note already specifies the runner history-capture extension needed. Extending it to $n\in\{10^4,10^5\}$ (Python history capture is feasible at $\sim$1 s/trial at $n=10^5$; the C++ engine would need a history-output flag, a small `main.cpp` change) would give the decisive $\sim6\times$ additional lever arm on the $n^{-0.4}$ prediction.
