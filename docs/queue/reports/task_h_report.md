## Question

Q3 clock-collapse clause: does the generational survival probability of the fear-only channel converge to the step-level geometric clock limit when generation sizes are $a_k = o(n)$? Per-generation bias measured as $\Delta P = (1-\mu/n)^{a_{k-1}} - (1 - \mu a_{k-1}/n)$ over all recorded cascade histories.

## Inputs

- `results/raw/fear_concentration_n{1000,2000,4000,8000}.json` — regenerated by `scripts/run_task_e.py` inside the overnight driver; runner-stamped (commit `fb0ac42`, base_seeds 202607041–4), histories present.
- Analysis: `twocascade.analysis.analyze_clock_collapse_bias` via `scripts/run_task_h.py`.

## Findings

**Clock-collapse CONFIRMED for $a_k = o(n)$.**

- **The bias collapses onto the ratio $a/n$:** at fixed $a/n$ the mean bias is $n$-independent across the 8× range of $n$ (e.g. $\approx 2\times10^{-4}$ at $a/n=0.05$ for every $n$; $\approx 0.010$–$0.014$ at $a/n=0.4$). It is a function of the generation-to-system ratio, not of $n$ itself.
- **In the $o(n)$ regime the bias is negligible:** max bias over all generations with $a/n \le 0.01$ is $\le 1.23\times10^{-5}$ at every $n$.
- **Macroscopic generations carry the only material bias:** the overall max bias per $n$ (0.028 → 0.049 as $n$ grows) occurs at the largest observed ratios ($a/n \approx 0.5$–0.66, the supercritical collapse generations) — a finite-$n$, macroscopic-generation effect exactly as expected ($\Delta P \to e^{-\mu\rho} - (1-\mu\rho)$ at fixed $\rho = a/n$).
